Take a Look Around

The Rooms of Marle Hall

A Grade-II listed 17th century house above the Conwy valley, with original Georgian features throughout. These are the principal rooms available to you and your guests.

Arrival

The Entrance Hall

A beamed entrance hall with original Georgian features, and the first impression your guests have of Marle Hall. It is a fitting place to welcome them as they arrive.

Ceremony

The Ceremony Room

A dedicated ceremony room with tall windows, chandeliers and original wooden floors, seating up to 80 guests.

Dining

The Dining Hall

A spacious dining room for your wedding breakfast or celebration dinner. Tables and chairs are provided, while the caterers, drinks and flowers are entirely your own choice.

Retreat

The Green Room

A quieter space for guests to gather, unwind and catch up between the moments of the day.

Gather

The Gallery

A gallery-style hallway hung with pictures, with a marble-tiled floor and bar stools and tables for guests to gather over a drink. A bar is planned for the future.
