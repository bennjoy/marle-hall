Welcome

A House Steeped
in Living History

A gorgeous Grade-II listed country house built in 1661, nestled on a hill within 30 acres of woodland. Set between the panoramic backdrop of the Snowdonia national park and the coastal sights of the West Shore, Marle Hall offers a truly unique setting for weddings and events.

From intimate ceremonies to grand celebrations, our historic hall and grounds provide a setting unlike anywhere else in North Wales.
